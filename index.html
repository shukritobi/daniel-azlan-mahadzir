A systems view of health

Healthspan is shaped long before someone enters a clinic.

It is shaped by food systems, environmental conditions, public trust, access to technology and the ability of institutions to move ideas into practice. Daniel's work connects these systems — from research and education to venture building and sustainable healthcare transformation.

About Daniel

Bridging science, policy and entrepreneurship.

Dr Muhammad Daniel Azlan Mahadzir is a global health innovation leader and researcher based in Singapore. His work combines scientific inquiry with programme design, venture building and public advocacy to create commercially viable, socially useful solutions.

Before his academic appointments, he worked in sustainable agriculture and renewable technology investment. That entrepreneurial background now informs his approach to translational research — asking not only whether an idea works, but how it can be adopted, funded and scaled.

He is also active in youth capacity development and climate action, including work with U-Inspire Alliance, UNESCO-linked networks and the World Economic Forum's Global Shapers community.
